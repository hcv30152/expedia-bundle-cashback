## What an Expedia vacation package actually is

Two ways to book, one checkout. Expedia gives you pre-built package deals (ready-made flight + hotel combos for specific dates), or you build your own: pick a hotel, resort, or vacation rental, then add flights, a rental car, or both. There's a dedicated all-inclusive filter if you want food, drinks, and activities rolled into the room rate — though terms differ by property, so read the resort's specifics before assuming meals and transfers are covered.

A few things the package price does and doesn't include:

- Displayed package prices are **per person**, based on double occupancy, the lowest room type available, and the lowest fare — hotel taxes included, other fees possibly not.
- **Baggage fees are often not included** in the airfare portion.
- The flight inside a package may be nonrefundable, nontransferable, and may not allow name changes.
- Promo-priced hotels can carry blackout dates.

That last group matters more than any headline discount. A package that saves you $60 but locks you into a nonrefundable fare is a worse deal than a slightly pricier flexible booking if there's any chance your dates move.

## How much do Expedia vacation packages really save?

The honest answer: it varies by property and season, and the advertised maximums are cherry-picked. The mechanism explains why. Hotels typically agree to discount rooms by roughly 10–25% in exchange for the room rate being hidden inside a bundle — that's the "opaque package" model, and it's the same reason Expedia Group can afford to keep an estimated 18–22% margin on standard hotel bookings. The hotel trades a deeper discount for not publicly slashing its rack rate.

So the numbers you'll see:

- **Up to 14%** off flight + hotel (Expedia's own bundle page advertising).
- **Up to $974** in Bundle & Save savings (Expedia's homepage headline — a maximum, not a typical figure).
- **About $47 average** savings per trip versus booking the same components separately (per Expedia internal data reported in 2025).
- A real-world example from Expedia's homepage: a 4-night San José, Costa Rica package with flights from Washington DCA at **$668 per traveler** at a 4.5-star property.

The practical move: run the comparison yourself. Search the flight and hotel separately, note the total, then price the bundle. Expedia itself says savings apply when compared to booking the same items separately at undiscounted, unrewarded rates — which means when a hotel is already running its own sale, the bundle advantage shrinks or disappears.

## FAQ

**Are Expedia vacation packages refundable?**
Partially, case by case. You can cancel through your itinerary page or customer service, and many hotels in packages offer fully refundable rates — but refund eligibility depends on how far from departure you cancel and the airline's fare rules. The flight component may be completely nonrefundable. Check the specific package's terms before paying, since the answer changes per booking.

**Do Expedia packages include checked bags?**
Usually not. The package price covers the room and the base airfare; baggage fees are generally paid to the airline separately. Factor $30–40 per bag each way into your comparison, or the bundle's savings number will flatter itself.
